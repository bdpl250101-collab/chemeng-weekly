  • Polyanion‐Modulated Vanadium‐Doped Ammonium Iron Phosphate Framework from Spent LiFePO 4 for Lattice Oxygen‐Participated Water Oxidation
    Advanced Energy Materials 2026-11-22 OER / HER
    Jiyoung Kim, JeongEun Yoo, Kiyoung Lee
    초록 보기

    Promoting the lattice oxygen mechanism (LOM) offers an effective route to improving the efficiency of water electrolysis. However, achieving efficient LOM operation in structurally stable catalysts remains challenging. To address this challenge, we report a microwave‐assisted upcycling strategy using spent LiFePO 4 as a lithium‐deficient precursor. The cycling‐induced lithium deficiency facilitates structural reorganization into a polyanion‐modulated vanadium‐doped ammonium iron phosphate hydroxide hydrate (S‐VAFP) without residual Li‐containing impurities. The resulting S‐VAFP features a three‐dimensional polyanionic framework composed of FeO 6 octahedra vertex‐linked with PO 4 tetrahedra, forming one‐dimensional channels that promote electrolyte accessibility and ion transport. This architecture tunes Fe─O covalency, weakens Fe─(O─P) linkages, and drives in situ reconstruction into a catalytically active amorphous phase, thereby facilitating lattice oxygen participation. Chemical probe experiments and 18 O isotope‐labeling DEMS further verify the AEM–LOM coupled OER pathway. Benefiting from this structure‐guided LOM activation, S‐VAFP delivers an overpotential of 250 mV at 100 mA cm −2 with a Tafel slope of 47.2 mV dec ‒1 . An assembled anion‐exchange membrane water electrolyzer (AEMWE) cell achieves 1.72 V at 1 A cm −2 with stability exceeding 1000 h. Overall, this work establishes polyanion‐mediated electronic modulation as an effective strategy for designing stable iron phosphate electrocatalysts with enhanced lattice oxygen participation.

  • Confinement Enables Direct Electrosynthesis of Acetaldoxime From CO and Nitrate
    Angewandte Chemie International Edition 2026-11-22
    Rongxing Qiu, Jiaran Li, Ke Chen 외 10명
    초록 보기

    Aldoximes are vital feedstocks in the fields of medicine, agriculture, and industry, yet their conventional synthesis suffers from high environmental costs and carbon footprints. Here, we report a direct electrocatalytic route to acetaldoxime via CO and NO 3 − co‐reduction.   • Electropositive Site Engineering via Ordered Intermetallic Electrocatalysts for Hydrogen Evolution: From M 2P to Zr2 M 12P7 ( M = Ni, Co, Fe)

    초록 보기

    Doping and alloying is a common strategy to modify the electronic structures of transition metal phosphide (M–P) electrocatalyst to improve their performance. However, the disorder associated with doping and alloying leads to a distribution of local crystal and electronic structures, thus complicating understanding of structure-property relationships. In turn, ordered ternary A–M–P phases with distinct crystallographic sites for each element represent tunable single-site catalysts given their diversity in compositions and structures. To maximize the effect of the second transition metal, the electropositive A = Zr was considered. The d-band center emerges as a useful descriptor of HER activity across both binary and ternary phosphides.

    Selective electrocatalytic CO2-to-C2H5OH conversion remains challenging, due to intricate multielectron transfer, complex C–C coupling, and Cu-based catalysts inherently favoring C2H4 over C2H5OH for lack of *OH-generating sites.     In this work, we deconvolute the impact of electrolyte cations on the electro-oxidation of glycerol (GOR) on Pt.
